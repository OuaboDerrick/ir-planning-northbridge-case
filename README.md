# Incident Response Planning: Northbridge Skills Institute Ransomware Case

A fictional-scenario incident response (IR) assignment completed for **GRC105 Incident Management and Business Continuity** at the International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA), Governance, Compliance, and Risk Training Program.

I act as Incident Response Lead for a training institute facing a suspected ransomware incident. The work analyses an evidence pack, builds a coordinated response plan, and tests the decisions in a documented tabletop exercise.

> **Note:** All organisations, systems, people, contact details and data in this repository are fictional. No real incident or real data is described.

## Scenario in brief

- **Organisation:** Northbridge Skills Institute (fictional), 1,800 learners and 90 staff.
- **Situation:** Monday 08:45 WAT. Finance workstation and file server show encryption and a ransom note. The learning portal is degraded. An online exam for 100 learners starts at 11:00.
- **Constraints:** Non-immutable, untested network backups; incomplete server EDR coverage; a three-person IT team and one security analyst; the incident contact list stored on the affected server.

## What is in the report

| Task | Deliverables |
|---|---|
| 1. Classification and initial assessment | Evidence-linked timeline (confirmed vs hypothesis vs unresolved), alternative explanations, severity criteria and rating, asset and CIA assessment, investigation priorities |
| 2. Plan and team | IR policy and plan outline, 8-role CSIRT, 10-activity RACI matrix, readiness checklist |
| 3. Playbook and evidence | 14-action playbook for the first 24 hours, evidence register with custody arrangements, backup validation, restoration order, recovery acceptance criteria |
| 4. Communication and escalation | Stakeholder communication matrix, executive briefing, learner notice, escalation rules, notification-assessment approach |
| 5. Tabletop exercise | 60-minute simulated exercise, five injects, role-by-role walk-through, decision log, results, improvement plan |
| 6. Case study analysis | Critical analysis of an illustrative healthcare ransomware case |

## Skills demonstrated

- Incident classification and severity-based prioritisation under uncertainty
- Evidence handling, order of volatility and chain of custody
- Containment decisions that balance forensics against business impact
- Backup validation and controlled, criteria-based recovery
- RACI design with a single accountable owner per activity
- Crisis communication and separating operational notices from formal breach notification
- Tabletop exercise design, facilitation and measurable improvement planning
